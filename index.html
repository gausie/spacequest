About

Sixteen scenes from Space Quest I: The Sarien Encounter (Sierra On-Line, 1986), captured the same way in every version of the AGI original and the 1991 SCI remake.

Not here yet: the black-and-white Macintosh AGI release and the Amiga SCI remake.

Captures are scaled to 1600x1200 with nearest-neighbour pixels, which also corrects 320x200 to 4:3.

Interface style after The Secret of Monkey Island: all 13 versions side by side by The Paninos off-line.
