crt.js: the CRT monitors of DOSBox Staging, for still pictures, in WebGL2

A port of the shaders behind DOSBox Staging's adaptive CRT settings (crt-auto, crt-auto-machine and crt-auto-arcade),
from its resources/glshaders/crt folder, version 0.82. https://www.dosbox-staging.org/
Ported to WebGL2 and changed for this site by The Paninos off-line, October and November 2026.
